## Conventions

- Keep generated or analysis reports under the relevant dataset folder.
- Use `baselines/` for cross-model summaries and runner reports.
- Use model-specific folders, such as `convvae/`, for readiness, integration, and result notes tied to one model.
- Use `ablation/` for ablation plans and ablation result notes.
